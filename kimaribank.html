Product Overview

Your go-to files, one click away. KIMARI BANK is a Photoshop plugin for registering the files you already use all the time — character bases, background textures, logos and watermarks, your own business card or packaging templates — and bringing them in with a single click, instead of digging through folders and copy-pasting every time.

Note: the plugin doesn't come with any pre-made assets or templates — everything you register is a file of your own.

Working in an open document? Click a preset and all its layers are pasted straight in. Starting something new from a template? Click with no document open, and the template's contents load into a fresh new document. Since your original template file is never opened directly, there's no risk of accidentally overwriting it. Just customize the new document and save it under whatever name you like.

Key Features:

• One-click register and apply: Supports PSD, PSB, PNG, JPEG, and TIFF. Just pick a file from your own library — that's it.
• Never overwrites your original file: Template files are never opened directly — they're always expanded into a brand-new document, so there's no risk of accidentally saving over your original.
• Easy re-linking: If a registered file goes offline because it's been moved from its original location, just point KIMARI BANK at a folder to re-link everything at once.
• Built for daily use: Cmd/Ctrl+drag to reorder, right-click to rename or delete, and a responsive grid that adapts to however wide you keep the panel.
• 100% local, no cloud: Everything is stored locally on your machine. Nothing is ever sent to an external server.

Who it's for:

・Illustrators and comic artists who reopen their own character base or template constantly
・Photo editors who paste in their own logo or watermark on every job
・Designers who produce business cards, packaging, or flyers from their own format
・Anyone who reuses the same personal assets across multiple projects
